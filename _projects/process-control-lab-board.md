## Overview

The lab kits for ECH4323L: Process Control Laboratory were built from an Arduino and a breadboard with hot-glued components, and they broke often. This board replaces them, consolidating the Arduino Uno and its external circuitry onto a single custom PCB smaller than the Uno itself.
